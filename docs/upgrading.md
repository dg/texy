# Upgrading texy/texy

## 4.0.0

- **Removed** `Texy\HandlerInvocation`: the event system is gone; transform the document by a pass registered with `Texy\Texy::addPass()` and change the HTML of a node by a renderer registered with `Texy\Output\Html\Config::addRenderer()` (07557c95)
- **Removed** `Texy\Parser`: a syntax handler receives a `Texy\Parsing\ParseContext` (07557c95)
- **Removed** `Texy\Modules\TableCellElement`: a table cell is a `Texy\Nodes\TableCellNode` of the document (07557c95)
- **Removed** `Texy\Modules\BlockModule`: modules are internal; the `/--` blocks are served by `Texy\Modules\DelimitedBlockModule` and their custom types by `Texy\Config::$delimitedBlockHandler` (212a2484, 5d1651f0)
- **Removed** `Texy\Modules\FigureModule`: modules are internal; figures are served by `Texy\Modules\ImageModule` (17d371d0)
- **Removed** `Texy\Modules\HorizLineModule`: modules are internal; horizontal rules are served by `Texy\Modules\HorizontalRuleModule` (212a2484)
- **Removed** `Texy\Modules\LinkModule`: modules are internal; links are served by `Texy\Modules\LinkReferenceModule` and `Texy\Modules\AutolinkModule` (4cc5500a)
- **Removed** `Texy\Modules\LongWordsModule`: hyphenation runs as a pass over the document, switched by `Texy\Config::$hyphenation` (212a2484, f2af7338)
- **Removed** `Texy\Modules\ScriptModule`: the `{{name: ...}}` directives are served by `Texy\Modules\DirectiveModule` and handled by `Texy\Config::$directiveHandler` (212a2484, 7130023d)
- **Removed** `Texy\Modules\TypographyModule`: typography runs as a pass over the document, switched by `Texy\Config::$typography` (f2af7338)
- **Removed** `Texy\Module::$texy`: custom modules are not supported; extend Texy with `Texy\Texy::addInlineSyntax()`, `Texy\Texy::addBlockSyntax()`, `Texy\Texy::addPass()` and `Texy\Output\Html\Config::addRenderer()` (07557c95)
- **Removed** `Texy\Texy::loadModules()`: modules are internal; affects subclasses overriding it (07557c95)
